========== KEY DECISIONS ==========

1. Decision: The European Union will study emissions data from suppliers from January 2026 to December 2026 to bring a cap to every product.
   Context: To monitor and regulate emissions from products imported into the EU, the European Union Commission has decided to collect data for two years starting from October 2025, specifically from January 2026 to December 2026, to establish a cap for each product.
   Responsible: European Union Commission

2. Decision: The European Union will implement a border adjustment mechanism, specifically the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), starting from October 2025, to monitor emissions from products imported into the EU.
   Context: The EU has drafted legislation requiring companies to disclose emissions from products imported into the EU on a quarterly basis, and the CBAM will be used to monitor and regulate these emissions.
   Responsible: European Union
