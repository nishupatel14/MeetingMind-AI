========== DISCUSSION TOPICS ==========

TOPIC: Introduction to Sustainability in the Healthcare Industry
  - The discussion revolves around the concept of sustainability, specifically in the context of the healthcare industry, with the example of Kiran Hospital, and how it can be applied to achieve a sustainable environment, social, and governance framework.
  - The main aim of preserving resources for future generations is highlighted as a key aspect of sustainability in this context.

TOPIC: History and Importance of Sustainability
  - The conversation touches on the history of sustainability, mentioning the 1997 protocol and the 2016 Paris agreement, which set targets for every country to limit the average global temperature increase to 2.5 degrees above pre-industrialization levels.
  - The importance of monitoring progress and taking steps towards sustainability is emphasized, with the mention of annual COP meetings, where countries' trajectories are assessed.

TOPIC: Environmental Compliance and Company Efforts
  - The discussion revolves around the topic of environmental compliance and the efforts of countries, particularly developing ones like India, to reduce their energy consumption and reliance on non-renewable sources.
  - The role of companies and industries in achieving environmental compliance is emphasized, with all companies working together to reduce their environmental impact.

TOPIC: ESG Reporting and Data Analysis
  - The conversation centered around companies' environmental sustainability and compliance, with the speaker mentioning that 90% of companies are already doing reports to stay compliant and pay taxes.
  - The importance of data analysis and reporting in the context of environmental sustainability is mentioned, with companies already collecting data and providing reports to investors.

TOPIC: Industry Growth and Government Support
  - The speaker highlighted the growth of the industry, mentioning that while it is not yet a big industry, it is already growing, with companies starting to manufacture environment-friendly products in countries like India.
  - The need for government support and help in addressing environmental and social issues is highlighted, with the mention of people wanting to work hard to keep the passion and the need for the presidency to provide help and guidance to companies and individuals working on these issues.

TOPIC: Environmental Impact and Operational Technology
  - The discussion revolves around the analysis of a framework for auditing and collecting data in an operational technology environment, specifically in the context of a power plant.
  - The conversation touches on the topic of environmental impact, mentioning the Bharat stage code, which indicates the level of emissions and mileage of a vehicle, and how it relates to the increasing implications of environmental concerns.
