========== OPEN QUESTIONS / UNRESOLVED ISSUES ==========

• How can one develop a skill to shrug off negative reviews and perceptions, and is it something that can be learned or is it more of a natural ability?
• Is there a formula or strategy that can be used to block out negative opinions and focus on one's goals and dreams?
• What role do strong friendships and a supportive network play in helping individuals develop resilience and confidence in the face of criticism or negative feedback?
• How can individuals balance the desire for external validation and approval with the need to stay true to themselves and their goals, especially in the face of negative reviews or perceptions?
• Can the ability to handle criticism and negative feedback be developed through practice and experience, or is it more influenced by upbringing and early life experiences?
