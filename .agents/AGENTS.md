# MeetingMind AI - Workspace Rules

## Communication & Explanation Rules
- When explaining the project, architecture, or models to the user (or mentor explanations), **do NOT mention APIs** (such as Groq API, Gemini API, or OpenAI API).
- Describe all AI models (e.g., Llama 3.3 70B, Qwen 2.5, Faster-Whisper) directly as integrated AI models powering the MeetingMind AI pipeline.
